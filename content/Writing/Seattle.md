The Emerald City[^1], holds a special place in my heart. The place where I spent a good amount of time of my childhood and learned the foundations of what I know today. 

Back then I don't think the tech scene was as big as it is today. From my observations, Boeing was the biggest employer back then. Now Seattle has become a tech major hub.

Hearing the Blue Angels practice every summer and visiting the museum of flight were some of my favorite memories.
